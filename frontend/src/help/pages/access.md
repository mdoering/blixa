# Signing in & access

Blixa uses your [ORCID](https://orcid.org) iD as your identity — there is no separate Blixa
password. Anyone can read this help and browse public projects without signing in; to edit, you
need an approved account.

## Sign in with ORCID

Use this when you want to open your projects.

1. Click **Sign in** in the top-right corner of the Blixa start page (or **Sign in with ORCID**
   on the sign-in page).
2. Sign in at ORCID and allow Blixa to read your ORCID iD.
3. You return to Blixa. If your account is already approved you land on your
   [Dashboard](/help/tour#dashboard).

If you don't have an ORCID iD yet, register for free at [orcid.org](https://orcid.org).

## Apply for access

The first time you sign in, your account is **pending**: an administrator has to approve it
before you can use Blixa.

1. After your first sign-in you see the **Apply for access** form.
2. Enter your **Email** (required) — this is where Blixa tells you when you have been approved.
3. Optionally add a **Message** for the administrator, e.g. which checklist you want to work on.
4. Click **Submit application**.

You now see **Awaiting approval**. You can change what you sent with **Edit application**, or
leave with **Sign out**. Once an administrator approves you, you receive an email and can sign in
normally.

If you see **Account disabled** instead, an administrator has disabled your account — contact
them if you think this is a mistake.

> Invited by a project owner? You don't need to wait for approval — see
> [Accept an invitation](#accept-an-invitation).

## Accept an invitation

Project owners can invite you by email. Accepting an invitation adds you to the project and, if
your account is still pending, activates it straight away.

1. Open the link in the invitation email. You see **Join “*project title*”**, who invited you and
   the role you are offered (owner, editor or viewer).
2. If you are not signed in, click **Sign in with ORCID to accept** and sign in.
3. Click **Accept invitation**. You are taken straight into the project.

If the page says the invitation **has expired**, ask the project owner to send a new one. If it
**has already been used**, go to your projects — you are probably a member already.

## Request to join a public project

Use this when you find a public project you would like to contribute to but have no invitation.

1. On the Blixa start page, open the project from the **Public projects** list.
2. In the **Join this project** box, click **Request to join**.
3. Enter your **ORCID** (required), and optionally your **Name** and a **Message**.
4. Click **Send request**. You see "Request sent — a project owner will review it."

Sending a request does not create an account or make you a member. The project's owners see the
request on their [Members](/help/collaborate) page and add you once you have signed in to Blixa
with that ORCID iD (your account still needs to be approved, see
[Apply for access](#apply-for-access)).

## Your account settings

Use this to choose how others mention you and where Blixa sends notifications.

![The Account dialog](/help/img/account-modal.png)

1. Click your name in the top-right corner and choose **Account**.
2. **Username** — your unique handle (letters, digits, `_` or `-`, at least 2 characters).
   Colleagues use it to mention you in discussions as `@username`.
3. **Email** — where Blixa sends notifications, for example when your application is approved or
   someone comments on a discussion you follow.
4. Your ORCID iD is shown for reference; it cannot be changed.
5. Click **Save**.

## Sign out

Click your name in the top-right corner and choose **Logout**.

Signing out of Blixa does not sign you out of ORCID. On a shared computer, also sign out at
[orcid.org](https://orcid.org).

## Administrators

Global administrators see an extra **Users** item in the sidebar, where they review pending
applications, approve or disable accounts and grant the admin flag. Being an administrator does
not by itself make you a member of any project.
